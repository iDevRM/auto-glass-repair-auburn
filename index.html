What Is Auto Glass Repair in Auburn?

Auto glass repair in Auburn covers every piece of glass on a car, truck or SUV. That means filling rock chips with resin, putting in a new windshield, replacing a side window or rear windshield, and re-aiming the safety camera that looks out through the front glass. Drivers on SR 167, SR 18 and Auburn Way share the road with a lot of trucks from the Kent Valley warehouses and the gravel pits out toward Black Diamond.

A windshield is a safety part too. It is glued to the body with urethane, it helps hold the roof up in a rollover and the passenger airbag pushes against it, so how it is installed matters as much as the glass itself.

How Much Does Windshield Replacement Cost in Auburn?

The cost of windshield replacement in Auburn depends mostly on what is built into the glass. Rain sensors, heated wiper areas, acoustic layers and heads-up display glass need more specialized parts, and a forward camera adds a calibration step. A plain windshield on an older sedan is a much simpler job than the glass on a new SUV loaded with sensors.

Insurance changes the math too. Washington has no zero-deductible glass law, so with comprehensive coverage a new windshield usually runs through your deductible, while many insurers waive it for chip repair. Some policies add full glass coverage, so it pays to check. Call with your year, make and model and we will give you a free quote and help you sort out your coverage before any work starts.

Frequently Asked Questions About Auto Glass in Auburn

The question we hear most on the phone is “Will my insurance pay for this?” It is the first answer below.

Does insurance cover windshield replacement in Washington?

Usually, if you carry comprehensive coverage. Washington has no law that waives the deductible on glass, so a new windshield normally goes through the deductible on your comprehensive coverage unless your policy adds full glass coverage. Many insurers waive the deductible on chip repair, which is a good reason to fix a chip while it is small.

Is it illegal to drive with a cracked windshield in Washington?

It can be. Washington law (RCW 46.37.410) requires that nothing on the windshield blocks the driver’s clear view of the road, and a crack across your line of sight can get you pulled over. A small chip off to the side is rarely a problem, but a long crack in front of the driver is.

Can a windshield chip be repaired instead of replaced?

Often it can. A chip smaller than a quarter, or a single crack shorter than about six inches, can usually be filled with resin if it sits away from the edge and out of the driver’s direct view. Tell us the size and where it is on the glass and we will say which fix fits.

Why do windshield chips spread in Auburn in the winter?

Cold glass and a hot defroster are the usual cause. On a frosty Auburn morning the outside of the glass is near freezing while the defroster heats the inside fast, and that pull spreads the chip. Sanded roads, studded tires from November 1 to March 31 and gravel trucks on SR 18 and SR 167 add new chips on top.

Do you come to my home or work?

Yes. Auburn Auto Glass Repair is a mobile service, so the technician works in your driveway, an apartment lot or the parking lot at your job. The address on this site is a service office, not a shop where you drop the car off.

How long does a windshield replacement take?

The install takes about an hour. After that the urethane adhesive needs roughly one to three hours before the car is safe to drive, and cold weather keeps it toward the long end. A chip repair is much quicker, usually about 30 minutes.

Can you replace a windshield when it is raining in Auburn?

Yes, as long as the glass and frame stay dry while the adhesive goes on. On rainy days we work in your garage, under a carport or under a canopy, and if none of those are available we pick a dry window in the forecast rather than rush the seal.
