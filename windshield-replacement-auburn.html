What Does Windshield Replacement Involve?

The job starts with covers over the hood, fenders and dash. The technician cuts the old glass free from its urethane bond and trims the old adhesive down to a thin, even layer. Next the frame gets dried, and any bare metal is primed so rust cannot start under the new seal, which matters with as much rain as Auburn gets from October to April. Then fresh urethane goes down, the new windshield is set and centered, and the mirror, sensors and moldings go back on. Plan on about an hour, plus cure time.

When Do You Need Windshield Replacement?

A crack longer than a dollar bill

Long cracks cannot be stopped with resin and keep running.

Damage in the driver’s view

Resin leaves a faint mark, so breaks in front of the driver need new glass.

A crack at the edge

Edge cracks weaken the bond between glass and frame and spread fast.

Several chips at once

Three or more chips, common for SR 18 and SR 167 commuters, add up to weak glass.

A leak in the rain

Water on the dash or a damp headliner often points to a failed seal.

Pitting that glares at night

Years of road sand leave tiny pits that make headlights on wet roads flare.

Why Do Windshield Cracks Spread in Cold, Wet Weather?

A windshield is two layers of glass with a clear plastic layer in between, and a rock chip breaks the outer layer. Glass shrinks as it cools and expands as it warms, and every swing pulls at the ends of the break. In Auburn the worst moment is a frosty morning when the defroster goes on full the second the car starts. Rainwater that soaked into the chip can also freeze overnight and pry it open. A chip picked up in October is often a crack across the glass by January.

Frequently Asked Questions

How soon can I drive after a new windshield?

Most urethane adhesives reach safe drive-away strength in one to three hours, and cold weather keeps it toward the long end. The technician gives you the exact time before leaving. Driving too soon can let the glass shift or weaken the bond the airbag relies on.

Is OEM glass better than aftermarket glass?

OEM glass comes from the same supplier that made your original windshield. Good aftermarket glass meets the same federal safety rules, but on cars with cameras or heads-up displays OEM glass can make calibration easier. We tell you which options fit your vehicle and your insurance.

When can I wash my car after a windshield replacement?

Wait at least a day before a car wash, and keep pressure washers away from the edges of the glass for a couple of days so the new seal is not disturbed. Normal rain is fine once the car is ready to drive.

Can I use the defroster right after a new windshield?

Yes, once the technician says the car is ready to drive. For the first few days, start it on a low setting instead of blasting hot air at cold glass.
