What Does Windshield Chip Repair Involve?

First the chip gets cleaned and any water inside is dried out, which is most of the battle on a damp Auburn day. A bridge tool is mounted over the damage with suction cups. It pulls the air out of the break, then pushes clear resin in under pressure. A UV lamp cures the resin hard, and the surface is scraped and polished flat. Most chips end up much harder to see, and they stop spreading.

When Do You Need Windshield Chip Repair?

A bullseye or star chip

Round and star-shaped chips under a quarter are the best candidates.

Short crack off a chip

A single crack under about six inches can often still be filled.

A chip before the first frost

Filling it in the fall keeps freezing water and the defroster from running it.

Leased car with a chip

A clean repair helps avoid a charge for damaged glass at turn-in.

Fresh hit from the highway

Stones from trucks on SR 18, SR 167 and SR 164 are the usual cause.

A chip outside the driver’s view

Repairs leave a faint mark, so they work best away from the driver’s eye line.

Why Does a Small Windshield Chip Turn Into a Crack?

A chip is a break in the outer layer of glass, and every change in temperature stresses its edges. Rain gets into the chip, freezes on a clear December night and pushes the break open from the inside. Then the defroster heats the glass fast the next morning. Potholes and slammed doors add shocks. People ask, “Can it wait until spring?” Sometimes. Through an Auburn winter, usually not.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a windshield chip repair take?

Most chip repairs take about 30 minutes on site, and you can drive right away. The resin is cured with UV light, so there is no waiting for it to dry.

Will the chip disappear completely?

Usually it gets much harder to see, but a faint mark often stays. The main goal is to stop the chip from spreading and give the glass back its strength.

Should I put tape over a chip until it is fixed?

Clear tape over the chip keeps rain and grit out until the repair. On cold mornings, ease the defroster up slowly instead of starting on full heat so the chip does not run.

Can a chip be repaired when it is raining?

Yes, as long as the chip is dried out first and the glass stays dry during the repair. The technician works under a garage, carport or cover so water does not get trapped under the resin.
